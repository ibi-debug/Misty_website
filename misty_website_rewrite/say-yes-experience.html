Seven Days

The Say YES Experience

Seven days. Decide. Commit. Move. A transformational container for people who are done circling and ready to operate from commitment, standards, and movement.

What This Is

Transformational, not motivational

This is where you interrupt Decision Drag with daily commitment. You are not collecting tips. You are installing a new way of deciding, protecting your yes, and following through.

How It Works

Decide → Commit → Move

Each day builds on the last: clear prompts, protected yeses, and a rhythm that keeps you out of the loop of “I will start Monday.” Inside the experience, you move from decision to commitment to visible momentum.

What Changes in Seven Days
  • Stronger self-trust through kept commitments
  • Cleaner decisions with less second-guessing
  • More movement, less hesitation
  • Clearer standards for what you will and will not negotiate

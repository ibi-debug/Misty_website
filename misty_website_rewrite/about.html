About Misty

Leadership changes the moment you stop negotiating with what you already know.

This body of work comes from decades of leading, building, and teaching — and seeing the same pattern repeat: capable people slowed down not by lack of skill, but by hesitation.

Why this work exists

I built this body of work because I kept seeing leaders stall in the same place: the gap between knowing and moving. More information was rarely the missing piece. The missing piece was understanding the pattern that kept the decision open.

"I don't build for applause. I build for the ripple."

My path started in North Battleford, Saskatchewan. Over the last three decades, I have led teams, built organizations, and taught leaders across industries. The through-line was always the same: decisions — how we make them, protect them, and teach others to lead themselves through them.

The Result

What I Help People Do

Reduce Decision Drag. Strengthen decisive leadership. Move with clearer standards, deeper self-trust, and momentum that compounds — without hype, noise, or performance for performance’s sake.

Where It Applies

Bigger Than One Industry

Small business, entrepreneurship, corporate teams, and network marketing — the same lens holds. Decision-Led Leadership is the umbrella; your arena is where you practice it.

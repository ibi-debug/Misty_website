The Story

"I don't build for applause. I build for the ripple."

She Was Never Meant to Live an Average Life.

My journey began in North Battleford, Saskatchewan, with a simple truth instilled by my parents: you do not have to be broken to be hungry for more.

I've spent 30 years in leadership and over 15 years in network marketing, building multi-seven-figure organizations. I played by the rules for a long time, but traditional corporate ladders always felt like well-decorated cages. I wasn't interested in climbing ladders that didn't lead to meaning.

"What a legacy she left for you."

Those words were spoken to me at my mother's funeral, and they redefined everything. I suddenly saw my work through a new lens. The mentorship and systems I was building were not just about income. They were about inheritance.

Now, I help high-performing women build their own legacies, balancing strategic luxury with unapologetic ambition. We build with soul, or we don't build at all.

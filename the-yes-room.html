What this is

A live conversation. Not a pitch deck.

  • Show up on Zoom. I'll share the arc of this work: the standards behind it, the decisions that built it, and how women step from knowing into movement.
  • If you've been circling the next level in your life or business, this is where the story becomes concrete, and you see where you might fit.
Why come

Context before commitment.

  • The YES Room is for women who don't want more noise. They want clarity on what this path actually requires, and what it gives back.
  • Bring your questions. Leave with a clearer read on whether you're ready to move, and what moving would look like for you.

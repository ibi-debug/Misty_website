Something clicked: leadership was not about control, it was about stewardship, about creating environments where others could remember their own power. That spark never left her.
As Misty moved into her early career, she played by the rules, but never truly belonged to them. Traditional roles and nine to five routines felt like well decorated cages. She was not interested in climbing corporate ladders that did not lead to meaning. Even when she excelled, she felt a voice in her gut saying: this is not it.
In 2010, Misty discovered the world of network marketing. She partnered with NuCerity International, not out of desperation, but from alignment. This was something she could build on her terms. While the industry was often filled with dramatic transformation stories, hers was different. She was not running from something, she was building toward something.
Over 17 years in network marketing and 30 years in leadership, Misty has built multi seven figure organizations and coached thousands to success. She has focused her career on network marketing, leadership, mindset, and mentoring women through growth and reinvention. Her ability to blend visionary strategy with emotional intelligence has made her one of the most trusted voices for women who are ready to redefine success on their terms.
Misty poured herself into the work with full belief. She did not just study products or strategies, she studied people. She mentored, recruited, trained, and most importantly, she listened. While others chased metrics, Misty built community. Her wins were rooted in integrity, empathy, and high standards. Her team did not just grow, it evolved. She made sure of it.
As the years unfolded, her leadership journey expanded. She later joined Tranont, where her mentorship and influence reached new levels. During this chapter, Misty refined her systems for empowerment, creating clear, repeatable frameworks that helped women lead with clarity instead of chaos. Her structured with heart leadership philosophy came fully alive, balancing precision with genuine care.
Her success with Tranont was not only measured in rank or accolades. It was reflected in the lives changed, the families stabilized, the confidence restored, and the quiet belief reignited in everyday leaders who thought they had lost their spark.
What most people do not see at a glance is the deeper foundation behind that success. Nearly three decades of personal and professional development shaped her approach to leadership. Long before she stood on stages or mentored teams globally, Misty was a student of growth, investing in herself through world class coaches, thought leaders, and entrepreneurial experiences that sharpened her ability to see what others miss. She did not just learn systems, she embodied mastery.
Behind every business win, Misty was also living a very human life. She was raising kids, running a household, supporting friends, and navigating real relationships. She was building a dream while carrying the invisible weight of wearing a hundred hats. There were days when everything looked put together, but inside it felt like she was holding it all together with thread. There were moments when she felt like she was doing everything, and yet never enough.
She often asked herself the quiet questions so many women carry: Who sees me? Who holds space for me while I hold space for everyone else?
Then came the moment that shook everything. Her mother passed away.
The grief was not only personal, it was deep and foundational. Her mother had been more than a parent, she had been a mirror, a living example of what it meant to lead with grace and give without asking for applause. At the funeral, someone walked up and said, "What a legacy she left for you." That sentence did not just land, it redefined everything.
Misty suddenly saw her work through a new lens. The mentorship, the leadership, the systems she was building were not just about income. They were about inheritance. A values based inheritance. A continuation of something sacred. From that moment forward, everything she built carried a different weight and a deeper purpose.
In 2015, she launched VIP Creators, a coaching and personal brand platform rooted in transformation from the inside out. Through this work, she partnered with women navigating reinvention, confidence, identity, and self worth. She coached them not only in business strategy, but in life strategy. They were not just finding clarity, they were finding themselves.
In 2022, Misty evolved again with ML Global Enterprises, also known as Global Legacy, a more expansive entity designed to serve both entrepreneurs and seasoned professionals who were ready to build legacy aligned lives and businesses. Through collaboration, mentoring, and impact focused consulting, Misty brought structure to the dreams of leaders around the world who were ready to scale from success to significance.

Nueva Life Compliance GuidelinesNueva Life Compliance Guidelines

When creating content, sharing personal stories, or promoting Nueva Life’s business opportunity, it is essential to avoid any language that suggests guaranteed income, financial independence, or exaggerated financial success. Such claims may mislead prospects and violate both Nueva Life’s internal policies and applicable regulatory standards (e.g., FTC, DSSRC).

General Financial Terms to Avoid
The following terms imply wealth, security, or financial outcomes that cannot be guaranteed and must not be used:
Financial Freedom
Financial Independence
Wealth Building
Millionaire
Passive Income
Fired My Boss
Retired from My Job

Specific Phrases to Avoid
These phrases may imply quick results, guaranteed success, or unrealistic expectations:
“Free Trip”
“#FinancialFreedom”
“In Position to Earn $__________”
“Easy Income”
“Quick Income”
“Quit My Job”
“Make Easy Money”
“Retirement Income”

Best Practice Language to Use Instead
Focus on effort-based potential and personal development. Messaging should reflect that success depends on individual action, consistency, and learning:
“Potential to earn based on individual effort and results”
“Opportunities to build an income stream through coaching and support”
“Tools to grow personally and professionally”
“Mentorship and community to help you develop your business”

Reminder
Your content must always be honest, balanced, and grounded in personal experience without implying typicality or certainty. When reviewing your message, ask yourself:
“Does this promise or suggest a financial outcome that cannot be guaranteed?”
